About HyperTerminal

    

      
☎ HyperTerminal online  - a tribute to the long-lost Windows serial terminal,
      rebuilt on the Web Serial API.

      
All data stays between your browser and your serial port - nothing is uploaded.

      
Works in Chrome and Edge on desktop. Firefox and Safari do not support Web Serial yet.

      
By ClearEvo -
      maker of the Bluetooth GNSS
      Android app (source on GitHub).
